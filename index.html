Featured Projects

Audio Plugins

ALMA is an all-in-one audio plugin developed in C++ and JUCE. Each of its 5 core macro modules handles a parallel chain of 3 independent effects, allowing users to toggle, stack, and fine-tune individual sub-parameters. It features an adaptive UI layer ("ALMA mode") that translates standard DSP parameters into intuitive terminology for beginners. It includes a factory bank and native custom preset serialization.

  • C++
  • JUCE
  • DSP
  • UI/UX (Figma)
  • 5 Macro modules
  • 15 Independent effects
  • 2 UI modes

TMVerb is an algorithmic stereo reverb developed in C++ and JUCE, built around a custom 8-line Feedback Delay Network: all-pass diffusers feed modulated delay lines with in-loop high-frequency damping and a Hadamard feedback matrix, keeping tails dense and smooth from tight rooms to 20-second ambient washes. Four main knobs (Pre-Delay, Decay, Tone and Mix) shape the core sound, while a three-mode XY pad controls six more parameters in pairs. It ships with 9 factory presets tuned for mono compatibility and stability from 44.1 to 96 kHz, plus custom preset saving.

  • C++
  • JUCE
  • DSP
  • FDN Reverb
  • Custom UI
  • 8 Delay lines (FDN)
  • 3 XY pad modes
  • 9 Factory presets
  • 20 s Max decay

Sueca

Card game · Windows

Sueca is the classic Portuguese card game, built from scratch in Godot. You and the bot across the table take on the other pair, following the ritual of a real table: shuffle, cut, and deal from the top or the bottom of the deck to set the trump. Matches go from 1 to 10 games, with traditional capote and bandeira scoring, a hand history and a built-in animated tutorial.

  • Godot 4
  • GDScript
  • Game AI
  • UI/UX
